Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-22

This policy explains how Local Rival handles information when you connect a game account, track a profile or join a local ladder.

Who to contact

Local Rival is the community gaming project maintained through EdugenIO. For privacy questions, access, corrections, deletion requests or complaints, email admin@edugen.io. Describe the issue without sending passwords, login codes or API keys. We may ask for reasonable proof that a request relates to your account.

Information we collect

  • Steam connection: your Steam ID, gamer name, connection and sync times, game profile details and available match statistics. Steam handles your password; we do not receive it.
  • Dota 2: match results, heroes, game modes, kills, deaths, assists and other available match metrics, provider snapshots and calculated local scores.
  • Location you choose: postcode and suburb/town, which determine your city, state and national ladders. We do not request GPS coordinates or your street address.
  • League trial: searched Riot IDs and regions, available public OP.GG match data, source timestamps and the profile you choose to save privately. Tracking does not verify Riot account ownership.
  • Account operation: hashed session credentials, short-lived login attempts, privacy preferences, and the policy version and time accepted when you join/save a profile.
  • Technical information: our hosting providers process connection details such as IP addresses and request logs. The application uses hashed network-address buckets in memory to limit abuse. Support emails contain the information you send us.

Why we use it

We use this information to sign you in, retrieve game data, save your profile, calculate and display opted-in Dota ladder scores, maintain account security, limit abuse, respond to requests and operate the service. Dota records can be refreshed manually and by the configured background sync. League trial profiles are searched on demand, cached briefly and saved only when you choose to save them.

What becomes public

Connecting Steam alone does not join a public ladder. Joining publishes your gamer name, state, match score, win rate, match count, recent result pattern and sync date. Suburb display is optional and off by default. Appearing in a postcode or city ladder reveals that general area even when suburb display is off. A reused gamer name may identify you. Other people can copy public information; leaving the ladder cannot remove copies they already made.

Saved League tracked profiles and private browser-imported Dota snapshots are not published to the ladder by those actions. Public information already available at Steam, OpenDota or OP.GG remains subject to those services.

Providers and overseas processing

Render hosts the application and PostgreSQL database; the deployment is configured in Singapore. Steam/Valve handles Steam sign-in, OpenDota supplies Dota data and OP.GG supplies League previews. Requests to these services include the game identifier needed for the lookup. Browser-based OpenDota sync also contacts OpenDota directly. Google Fonts and Steam-hosted sign-in artwork can receive your IP address and browser request details when your browser loads them. GitHub hosts the open-source code, the Pages redirect and any configured sync workflow.

These providers may process information outside Australia, including Singapore, the United States, South Korea and other locations described in their own policies. We do not control their independent records, retention or availability. The current application does not include advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your account or location data. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to address abuse or a security incident.

Cookies, storage and security

Local Rival uses essential first-party cookies: a secure sign-in session lasting up to 30 days and a Steam login-state cookie lasting up to 10 minutes. They are inaccessible to page scripts. Signing out invalidates the current session; it does not delete your account. Server access checks, request limits and cross-site protections help protect saved information. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

We retain saved account, match and location records while you keep them in Local Rival. Login/session records expire and are cleaned during relevant account operations; expiration may precede physical cleanup. League lookup cache entries are served for up to 15 minutes and may remain in bounded process memory until replaced or the process restarts. Hosting logs and backups follow provider retention arrangements; we do not promise instant erasure from backups.

  • Use My player profile → Download my data to export your saved data while signed in.
  • Edit your ladder profile to change your self-declared location or suburb visibility, or leave the ladder to remove your live public listing.
  • Remove a saved League profile in the League trial.
  • Use Disconnect & delete data in your account controls to remove your Local Rival Steam link, saved game data, League profile, ladder membership, recorded policy acceptances and account sessions from the active database.

These controls do not delete your Steam/Riot accounts or source data held by other providers. Support correspondence, infrastructure logs, backups and copies made by others may remain. Contact us for access or correction assistance, or if you cannot sign in. If you make a privacy complaint, we will investigate and respond; where applicable you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Updates

We will update this page when practices change. Material changes will be highlighted in the application or an appropriate notice. New join/save actions require the current terms version. Privacy obligations and your rights depend on applicable law; this policy does not limit them.
