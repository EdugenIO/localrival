Postcodes are self-declared. Do not claim a false home location to manipulate a ladder or publish someone else’s private location. A public postcode/city listing reveals your general area. Review the sharing notice before joining. You can edit your location, stop optional suburb sharing or leave.

Dota local points are community activity/performance scores based on available results and KDA, using the methodology shown in the app. They are not official skill ratings, proof of ability, eligibility for prizes or a guarantee of rank. We may correct errors and revise the published method with notice appropriate to the change. Provider delays, missing matches, game modes and small samples can affect results.

The League profile trial previews available OP.GG statistics. It does not currently create public League ladder entries, custom points, verified Riot ownership or nightly League refreshes.
